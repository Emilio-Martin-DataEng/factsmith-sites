Welcome

A small school where every learner is known

The Word of God forms the basis of all aspects of the school, and every subject is taught from a Biblical worldview. Character development is at the core of what we do: honesty, perseverance, confidence, compassion, respect and understanding.

We are fully registered as a private school under the Independent Schools Act, and the education we offer meets the requirements of the National Curriculum Statement.
