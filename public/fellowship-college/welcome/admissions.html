Admissions

Joining Fellowship College, one step at a time.

Fellowship College is a small, WCED-registered independent Christian school in Melkbosstrand. Here is how enrolment works, step by step.

How to enrol

Five steps

  1. Enquire. Message the school on WhatsApp (or call 021 553 0374) with your child's name and current grade.
  2. Visit. See the school day and how modules work, and ask your questions in person.
  3. Apply. Send the school your child's details and the documents it asks for.
  4. Placement. Interview or placement assessment so your child starts at the right level. TBC
  5. Place confirmed. Sign the financial policy, get the uniform and stationery lists, and you're in.

Learning

How mastery-based learning works

Own pace, real understanding

Learners work through modules and only move on once they are proficient. A quick learner is not held back; a learner who needs more time gets it, instead of being pushed on with gaps.

Goals every day

Students set daily and term goals and track them with their teacher. The habit of planning and finishing work carries into matric, university and work.

Grades and exams

Grades 1 to 12 (75 learners, 2025 figures) to confirm. Learners write IEB exams, and the education meets the National Curriculum Statement. Subjects offered TBC.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is Fellowship College a registered school?

Yes. Fellowship College is registered with the Western Cape Education Department as an independent school under the Independent Schools Act, and its education meets the National Curriculum Statement.

How does mastery-based learning work?

Learners work through modules at their own pace and move on once they are proficient in the work. They set and meet their own goals with a teacher's guidance.

Is Fellowship College a Christian school?

Yes. The Word of God forms the basis of all aspects of the school, and every subject is taught from a Biblical worldview.

Which exams do matrics write?

Grade 12 learners write the Independent Examinations Board (IEB) National Senior Certificate.
