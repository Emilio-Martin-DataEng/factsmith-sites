Surf check

Before and after school

Melkbos faces west, so east and south-east winds blow offshore and clean up the waves. Each school day is rated at 07:00 and 15:00.

Good = waves 0.8 to 2.5 m, swell period 10 s or more, and wind offshore or under 15 km/h. Fair = surfable but messy or small. This is a forecast model, not a surf report. Check conditions yourself and never surf alone.
