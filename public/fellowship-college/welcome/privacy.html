Privacy and cookie notice

What this website does with your information, what cookies it uses and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated 30 October 2026. This is a draft for the school to approve before the site goes live. Items marked TBC are waiting on the school.

1. Who is responsible

Fellowship College, 01 Newark Street, Melkbosstrand 7441, is the responsible party for personal information on this website. Phone 021 553 0374, email principal@thefellowship.co.za.

Information Officer: name TBC · contact TBC.

The website was built and is maintained for the school by SELECT STAR DATA ENGINEERS (Pty) Ltd, trading as FactSmith Sites, which acts as the school's operator and handles information only on the school's instructions.

2. What information the website handles

  • Enquiries. The enquiry form does not send or store anything on this website. It opens your own WhatsApp or email with your message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. If you do, the school receives your name, contact details and message, and uses them only to answer you.
  • WhatsApp. If you chat to the school on WhatsApp, your number and messages are held by the school and by WhatsApp (Meta).
  • Photos. Photos of learners are shown only with the consent of a parent or guardian, and without full names beside them. Staff appear only with their own consent.
  • Dates, fees and notices. These come from a spreadsheet the school owns. It holds no learner or parent details.
  • Visitor statistics. TBC with the school. At the moment no analytics are installed. If the school switches them on, this notice will be updated first and a choice will be offered.

3. Cookies

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. This website sets no cookies of its own. It remembers that you closed the cookie notice in your browser's local storage, which is not a cookie and is not sent anywhere.

Some pages load content from other companies, which may set their own cookies or record your IP address when the page opens:

Google Maps and Google EarthMaps on the Contact and Community pages, and the Google Earth fly-in links
Google FontsThe typeface used on every page
WindyThe live wave map on the Weather page
Open-MeteoWeather, sea and tide figures, loaded in your browser
FactSmith conditions widgetThe surf and kite forecast on the Weather page
WhatsApp (Meta)Only if you tap a WhatsApp button

These companies' own notices apply to what they collect. Your browser lets you block or delete cookies at any time; the maps and weather may then not show.

3a. Why and for how long

Information is used only to answer your enquiry, run the school and keep this website working. It is kept only as long as that needs, and enquiries on WhatsApp or email are deleted by the school when no longer needed. retention period TBC

4. Sharing and other countries

The school does not sell personal information. The services listed above, and the website's hosting, run partly on servers outside South Africa.

5. Children

Information about a child is used only with the consent of a parent or guardian. A parent can withdraw consent at any time and the photo will be removed from the website within 7 days.

6. Keeping it safe

This is a static website with no database and no login, so there is nothing for visitors to sign in to or for a hacker to extract from the website itself. The school and its operator keep the website files secure, and will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected if a breach touches personal information.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask the school to:

  • tell you what personal information it holds about you or your child,
  • correct it, or delete it where the law allows,
  • stop using it, or object to how it is used.

Email principal@thefellowship.co.za or message the school on WhatsApp (082 460 4878). If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

8. Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
