About us

An independent Christian school on the West Coast, built on the Word of God and focused on character development.

Who we are

Who we are

Fellowship College is an Independent Christian School based in Melkbosstrand. We are registered with the Western Cape Education Department.

We use a personalized system of instruction in which students work and progress at their own pace through modules. This system is mastery-based: Students are trained to set and meet goals, and to become proficient in the work they undertake.

The Word of God forms the basis of all aspects of the school. Therefore, all subjects are taught from a Biblical world-view.

Character development is at the core of our educational focus. Honesty, perseverance, confidence, compassion, respect, and understanding are traits that we strive to see developed in all our learners.

What grows here

Every learner grows in six ways. Each leaf is a trait the teachers work on with them, a little more each day, at the learner's own pace.

With a total of 75 students from Grade 1 - 12 and 10 full-time, qualified teachers, plus 6 classroom assistants, classes stay small. This ensures that all learners get individual attention, and that good relationships are cultivated between all staff and learners.

Curriculum

Fellowship College is fully registered as a private school in accordance with the Independent Schools Act of South Africa. The education offered meets the requirements of the National Curriculum Statement of Education in South Africa.
