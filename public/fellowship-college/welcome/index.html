Questions parents ask

Is Fellowship College a registered school?

Yes. Fellowship College is registered with the Western Cape Education Department as an independent school under the Independent Schools Act.

How does learning work at Fellowship College?

Learners work through modules at their own pace and move on once they have mastered the work. Students learn to set and meet their own goals.
