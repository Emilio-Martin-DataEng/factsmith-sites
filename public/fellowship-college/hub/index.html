Faith and character

The Word of God forms the basis of the school, and every subject is taught from a Biblical worldview. We grow honesty, perseverance, confidence, compassion, respect and understanding.

How learning works

Learners progress through modules at their own pace and move on once they are proficient. They learn to set and meet goals, and the education meets the National Curriculum Statement.
