Fellowship College: three sample layouts

Three directions for a new school website, each modelled on a type of school site that works well. Same facts, photos and logo in all three. Dates, grades and fees marked TBC are waiting on the school.

A. HeritageTraditional independent school: crest, navy and red, full photo hero, quick links, mandate verse, news cards.
B. WelcomeWarm and simple: one clear "Book a visit" button, values, how learning works, parent FAQ.
C. Parent hubModern and practical: key facts up front, next-date strip, term and exam calendar, school life.
Getting started: photo and content registerWhere each photo and piece of content goes, how to name your files, and how to send them (Smash for bulk photos, email for the rest).
