Photo and content register

Every photo and every piece of content has a named place on the new site. Name your files after the place, send them in, and we tick them off here.

How to send things

1. Name the fileUse place-short-description.jpg: lower case, hyphens, no spaces. Example: classroom-grade-10-maths.jpg. If it fits no place, start it with extra- and tell us what it shows.
2. Many photos: SmashUpload them at fromsmash.com (free, works on a phone or computer) and send the transfer to emilio@factsmith.co.za, or paste the link in the WhatsApp group.
3. Everything else: emailPDFs, forms, lists and short texts go as attachments to emilio@factsmith.co.za. Dates, fees and activities go in the school's Google Sheet (we set it up).

Learner photo consent is confirmed. Photos can be camera or phone; send the original, we make the web copies.
