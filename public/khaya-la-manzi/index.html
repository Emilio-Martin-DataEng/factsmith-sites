Khaya La Manzi Guest Lodge

Sea-view guest lodge in Hibberdene

Khaya La Manzi means "home of water". A sub-tropical garden with 180° of Indian Ocean, a short walk from Hibberdene's patrolled beach and 2.5 km off the N2.

On the doorstep

Hibberdene & the South Coast

  • Hibberdene main beach. Lifeguard-patrolled, wide and safe, with a lagoon where the kids paddle upstream.
  • Surfing. Beach breaks right along the South Coast.
  • Fishing. Rock and surf fishing at sunrise, and charters from the beach launch.
  • Diving. Aliwal Shoal and Protea Banks are within reach for divers.
  • Golf. A string of South Coast courses within a short drive.

FAQ

Questions guests ask

Where is Khaya La Manzi?

158 Riviera Highway, Hibberdene, KwaZulu-Natal, 4220. 2.5 km off the N2 on the KZN South Coast, about an hour south of Durban.

Is Khaya La Manzi a B&B or self-catering?

Both. Bachelor and family rooms run B&B style (dinner by arrangement), and there are three self-catering sea-facing apartments plus a three-bedroom self-catering lodge.

Do the rooms have sea views?

The lodge looks out over the Indian Ocean with 180° views. Elephant, Seahorse, Whale and the Lodge are sea-facing.

Is Khaya La Manzi close to the beach?

Yes. Hibberdene's lifeguard-patrolled main beach is a short walk downhill, next to the river lagoon.
