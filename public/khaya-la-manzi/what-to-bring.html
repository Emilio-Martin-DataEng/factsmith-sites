What to bring

We supply the linen, a bath towel each and the kitchen basics. Here is what to pack for the rest.

  • Your favourite pillow if you have one, and extra blankets if you feel the cold. We supply two pillows per person, a duvet and sheets.
  • A beach umbrella, sun block and a rain umbrella.
  • A two-pin plug for your phone charger.
  • A hair dryer. We have one to borrow at reception if you need it, and an iron for guests: just ask.
  • Beach and pool towels. We supply one bath towel per person; please don't take them to the pool or beach. Beach towels can be hired for R50 per guest per stay.
  • Bottled water, if that is what you drink at home: better not to switch on holiday. There is a mineral water bottling plant about 3 km away where you can buy or refill, and reverse osmosis water at Spar.
  • Your medication. If you forget, there is a very helpful little chemist in Hibberdene.
  • Mosquito repellent.

Above all else, travel safely, and don't waste money on speeding tickets: watch out for the traffic cameras over the holiday season.

Check-in is from 14:00 to 19:00. Running late? Let us know on WhatsApp and don't stress.
