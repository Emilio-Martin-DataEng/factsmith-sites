Khaya La Manzi Guest Lodge

Terms & conditions

General

  • Right of admission is reserved.
  • The owner cannot be held responsible for any loss of or damage to property or possessions, or injury, sustained by a guest or visitor, whether caused by fire, theft or otherwise, or by negligence of the owner or a wrongful act by anyone in the owner's employment.
  • We are a private facility: for the security of all guests, no visitors are allowed on the premises.
  • One bath towel per guest is provided. Please bring beach and swimming towels and any other extras you need.
  • All tariffs and terms are subject to change without prior notice.
  • Key collection is between 14:00 and 17:00 daily. Units must be vacated by 10:00 on the day of departure.
  • Late key collection is by arrangement, for a fee, when possible. Late arrivals who did not make an arrangement may not be able to collect keys, and no refunds will be given.
  • Late departures may be arranged with reception beforehand, subject to availability; a day charge applies. Keys are to be left at reception on departure.
  • No pets may be brought onto the property under any circumstances.
  • The communal games room and swimming pool close at 22:00 each day.

Self-catering

  • One toilet roll per bathroom is provided; please bring extras, cleaning materials and dishcloths.
  • Daily servicing can be arranged with management for R100 per day (R250 for the Lodge): sweeping and mopping, washing dishes, cleaning bathrooms, making beds, removing rubbish and cleaning braais. No servicing on Sundays and public holidays.
  • Minimum rates and stays apply to the apartments; send your dates for a quote. The number of guests is determined by the number of beds.
  • A refundable key and breakage deposit of R500 applies (R2 000 for the Lodge), refunded within 5 working days of departure.

The Lodge

  • Khaya La Manzi is in a residential area: if the police are called for excessive noise or disturbance, your deposit is forfeited.
  • Other guests stay on the property. Unless you have booked the whole facility, keep noise down at all times. Planning a big celebration? Consider booking the whole property.

Payment

  • A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to confirm your booking.
  • Out of season, the balance is payable in full 5 working days before arrival. In season, the balance is payable in full 3 weeks before arrival.
  • Non-payment of any amount by the due date is taken as automatic cancellation, unless a written arrangement has been made with management.

Cancellation

  • 30% of the booking value is forfeited where a refund applies.
  • Peak, off-peak and long-weekend cancellations: no refunds unless an alternative booking fills the vacated dates.
