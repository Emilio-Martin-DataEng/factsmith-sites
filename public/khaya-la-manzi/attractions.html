Attractions

Beaches and rock pools on the doorstep, Zulu heritage at Umzumbe, and family days out up and down the coast.

Hibberdene is a quiet beach town, so the best days out are a short drive away: north to Mtwalume, Elysium and Ifafa, or south through Umzumbe and Banana Beach to Umtentweni and Port Shepstone. Distances are approximate, from the lodge.
