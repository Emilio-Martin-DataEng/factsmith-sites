Attractions

Beaches on the doorstep, and family days out up and down the coast: our own list.

Hibberdene is a quiet beach town in the middle of the South Coast, so the best days out are a drive away in either direction. A longer list of things to do, including hikes, is at reception: just ask. Distances are approximate, from the lodge.

Beaches

About 800 m

Hibberdene main beach

A clean little swimming beach with lifeguards, across the main road in front of the shops. It can be a bit rough, but it is very convenient.

Photos & map
About 6 km

Umzumbe Beach

The next closest, with lifeguards. No river mouth, so the water is clean, and there is a small tidal paddle pool for the kids. No shops, and quieter on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

Photos & map
About 30 km north

Pennington

A quiet beach with a tidal pool, and the conservancy coffee shop above it serving reasonable, tasty meals.

Photos & map
About 40 km north

Scottburgh

A very nice, child-friendly beach with a big grass area.

Things to do

About 6 km

Gold Reef Equitation Centre, Umzumbe

Horse riding for all ages: lessons, or outrides on the beach or in the forest. Turn right by the old abandoned convent and ask for Sheralee.

Photos & map
About 10 km

Upside Down House

A walk-through house built completely upside down: a quirky photo stop the kids love.

Photos & map
About 45 km south

Margate

Most of the night clubs on the coast. Not the Margate of old, but still a good place to visit and to fish.

Photos & map
45 km and more, south

Margate and Port Edward side

Waffle House, Mac Banana, Eland Resort, Oribi Gorge and Wild 5 Activities (zip lines and more): all on our must-see list.

Photos & map
About 100 km north

uShaka Marine World, Durban

Generally a must for children over the holidays.

Photos & map
About 75 km north

Splash Water World, Amanzimtoti

11 slides and water tubes on Amanzimtoti beach. An entrance fee applies.

Photos & map
About an hour south

Wild Coast Sun, Port Edward

A similar water world at the casino resort, but over the holidays access is often limited by numbers, and sometimes to hotel guests only.

Golf

We are fairly central to all the South Coast courses, from Umkomaas to the Wild Coast Sun. There is a brochure at reception.

Casinos

Durban and Port Edward, each about an hour away in opposite directions.

Diving and snorkelling

Diving and snorkelling at Umkomaas and Rocky Bay, with Aliwal Shoal for shark cage and baited shark dives; more experienced diving at Protea Banks.

Shopping and malls

Walking distance

Hibberdene shops

A small Pick n Pay, Superspar, Tops, hairdressers, a few beach stores and an all-sorts shop. Meat and alcohol prices tend to go up in December.

Photos & map
About 35 km south

Shelly Beach Mall

Pick n Pay, Woolworths, a few restaurants and shops, and small cinemas that fill up on rainy days.

Photos & map
About 35 km south

South Coast Mall

Just up the road from Shelly Beach Mall: Checkers, Food Lover's Market, and restaurants such as Ocean Basket, Mugg & Bean and Milky Lane.

Photos & map
About 40 km north

Scottburgh Mall

Pick n Pay, Woolworths, Dis-Chem, clothing stores and a Wimpy. An easier trip in holiday traffic.

Photos & map
About 70 km north

Galleria, Amanzimtoti

All the big names, restaurants, several cinemas, ten-pin bowling, go-karts and an arcade, 45 minutes away on the freeway. Makro and a large Pick n Pay hyper are just along the road. Our choice in peak season.
