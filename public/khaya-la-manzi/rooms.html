FAQ

Choosing a room

Which unit suits a family?

Family unit 5 sleeps 6, Dolphin sleeps 5, and Family unit 4 and Elephant sleep 4. For a bigger group, book the three-bedroom Lodge.

Are the rooms air-conditioned?

The bachelor and family rooms are air-conditioned. Air-conditioning in the self-catering apartments is to be confirmed.

Is there TV?

Each unit has a TV with SABC channels and a DStv connection: bring your own decoder and smart card.

What are the rates?

Rates depend on season and length of stay. See live rates and availability on the Book online page, or ask for a quote.
