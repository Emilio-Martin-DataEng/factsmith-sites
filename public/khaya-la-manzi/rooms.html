FAQ

Choosing a room

Which unit suits a family?

Family units 4 and 5 and Elephant each sleep 4. Units 4 and 5 share a covered, enclosed back patio, so they suit two families travelling together. For a bigger group, the three-bedroom Lodge sleeps 6.

Are the rooms air-conditioned?

Yes. The bachelor and family rooms and all the self-catering apartments are air-conditioned.

Is there TV?

Yes. Every unit has a TV, with DStv or Google TV depending on the unit, and every Lodge bedroom has its own TV.

What are the rates?

Rates depend on season and length of stay. See live rates and availability on the Book online page, or ask for a quote.
