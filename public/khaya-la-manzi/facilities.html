Facilities

Everything for an easy stay

Pool, braais, games room, gym and secure parking for every guest.

Braais everywhere. Seahorse and Elephant have built-in braais on their sea-facing decks, family units 4 and 5 share a covered back patio, and the Lodge has several sea-facing decks.
Good to know

Included in every stay.

  • Daily servicing, free of charge (not Sundays or public holidays)
  • Free WiFi
  • Air-conditioning
  • All linen, and a bath towel per guest
  • Starter pack of coffee, tea, Cremora and sugar on arrival
  • Soap, shampoo and lotion on arrival
  • All the crockery and cutlery you need for self-catering
  • Secure parking with CCTV

Please bring your own beach and pool towels. Swimming towels are available to hire if needed: R50 per guest per stay. What to bring

Breakfast

Breakfast includes:

  • A mini buffet of cold options: a selection of cereals, seasonal fruit, yoghurts, cheese and tomato slices, avo (in season), scones or muffins, nuts, jams and honey
  • A hot plated breakfast: 2 eggs, sausage, fried tomato, mushrooms and 2 slices of bacon
  • A third protein of the day (boerewors, chicken livers, a chicken patty, fish and so on), toast, fruit juice, coffee and tea

The selection may vary according to season and availability.

House rules, briefly.

  • Check-in 14:00 to 19:00 (later by arrangement); check-out 10:00
  • Pool and games room close at 22:00
  • No pets, no day visitors
  • Refundable key and breakage deposit

FAQ

About the facilities

Is there a swimming pool?

Yes, with decks and a braai area. Children must be supervised by an adult.

Is there secure parking?

Yes, on site behind a remote-controlled gate, with CCTV on the parking area. Please close the gate behind you for everyone's security.

Is there something for the kids to do?

The games room has pool, table tennis and board games. It is open until 9 pm and children must be supervised.

Is there a gym?

Yes, a small gym for guests.
