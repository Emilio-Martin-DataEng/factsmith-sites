FAQ

About the facilities

Is there a swimming pool?

Yes, with decks and a braai area. The pool is not fenced or netted, so children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

Is there secure parking?

Yes, on site behind a remote-controlled gate. Please close the gate behind you for everyone's security.

Is there something for the kids to do?

The games room has pool, table tennis and board games. It is open until 9 pm and children must be supervised.

Can we hold a conference or group event?

Yes. Tell us your dates and numbers and we will send a quote. Venue capacity and equipment TBC.
