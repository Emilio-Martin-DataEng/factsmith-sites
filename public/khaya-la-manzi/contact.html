Directions

Finding us.

From Durban (N2 south, about 1 hour)

Take the Hibberdene R102 exit (old Main Road). Turn left at the bottom of the ramp and continue about 2 km. Turn right into David Drive and follow the Khaya La Manzi signs.

From Port Shepstone (N2 north)

Take the Hibberdene exit and turn right at the bottom of the ramp. Continue about 2 km, turn right into David Drive and follow the signs.

Scenic route along the R102

From Port Shepstone follow the R102 for about 22 km, crossing the Mzimayi River bridge into Hibberdene. At the traffic lights turn left and immediately right into Riviera Highway. Go past the police station, up the hill for 500 m: Khaya La Manzi is on the left.

GPS: 30.5749° S, 30.5726° E (30° 34′ 29.6″ S, 30° 34′ 21.4″ E)

FAQ

Booking questions

What are check-in and check-out times?

Key collection is between 14:00 and 17:00. Units must be vacated by 10:00 on the day of departure. Late key collection is by arrangement, for a fee.

How do I pay?

A 50% non-refundable deposit confirms your booking. Out of season the balance is due 5 working days before arrival; in season, 3 weeks before.

Are pets allowed?

No. Pets are not allowed on the property.

How do I get to Khaya La Manzi from Durban?

Take the N2 south and the Hibberdene R102 exit. Turn left at the bottom of the ramp, go about 2 km, turn right into David Drive and follow the Khaya La Manzi signs.
