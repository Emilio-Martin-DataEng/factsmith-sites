Khaya La Manzi Guest Lodge

Sea-view guest lodge in Hibberdene

Khaya La Manzi means "home of water". A sub-tropical garden with 180° of Indian Ocean, a short walk from Hibberdene's patrolled beach and 2.5 km off the N2.

On the doorstep

Hibberdene & the South Coast

  • Hibberdene main beach. Lifeguard-patrolled, wide and safe, with a lagoon where the kids paddle upstream.
  • Surfing. Beach breaks right along the South Coast.
  • Fishing. Rock and surf fishing at sunrise, and charters from the beach launch.
  • Diving. Aliwal Shoal and Protea Banks are within reach for divers.
  • Golf. A string of South Coast courses within a short drive.
