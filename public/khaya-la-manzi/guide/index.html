Your stay

Arrival, departure and house rules

  • Check-in 14:00 to 19:00 (later by arrangement). Running late? WhatsApp us and don't stress.
  • Check-out by 10:00. Please leave your keys at reception.
  • Free daily servicing, not on Sundays or public holidays. Wet or dirty towels are swapped every day.
  • Pool and games room close at 22:00. Children must be supervised.
  • No pets and no day visitors, for everyone's security. Please close the gate behind you.

Breakfast

  • A hot plated breakfast: 2 eggs, sausage, fried tomato, mushrooms and 2 slices of bacon
