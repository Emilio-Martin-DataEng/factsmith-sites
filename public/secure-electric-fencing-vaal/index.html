Selling your property?

Electric fence compliance certificates

Since 1 October 2012, any property with an electric fence must have a valid Electric Fence System Certificate of Compliance before ownership can transfer. The certificate is issued by a registered electric fence installer and covers the whole system: energiser, wires, earthing, gate contacts and warning signs.

Common questions

Short answers to what customers ask most.

Why does my electric fence keep beeping?

The energiser is warning you of a fault: usually plants or debris touching the wires, a broken strand, a weak battery after load-shedding, or a poor earth. Clear anything touching the fence, then call us if it continues.

What does a gate motor repair cost?

It depends on the fault. Many repairs are a new battery, remote or gear set. Send a photo or call and we will give you a free quote. Add typical prices once confirmed

Do I need an electric fence certificate to sell my house?

Yes. Since October 2012 a valid Electric Fence System Certificate of Compliance is required before a property with an electric fence can transfer.
