UPDATED WEBSITE

What we build

A fast, mobile-friendly site plus a clean-up of the business's listings.

The website

  • Home page that says in one line what they do and where, leads with 'Since 2000', with call and WhatsApp buttons always in reach
  • A page for each service: electric fencing, gate motors and gates, garage doors, alarms, CCTV and intercoms, welding and repairs
  • A page for each area: Vanderbijlpark, Vereeniging, Sasolburg, Meyerton and the surrounding Vaal
  • Our story: the founding in 2000 and the growth into their own premises, told as proof of reliability
  • Compliance certificate page, photo gallery and a quote form that goes straight to their phone
  • Secure connection (https), so no browser labels the site 'Not secure'
  • A family link: the Vaal and Jeffreys Bay sites link to each other as one family business

The listings clean-up

  • Set up or claim the Google Business Profile for the Vaal premises
  • Merge the duplicate Procompare profiles and update old directory entries
  • Settle on one phone number and one email, used everywhere
  • Ask happy customers for Google reviews after each job

Getting found: SEO, AEO and GEO

People find a local business three ways: they search Google, ask a question and expect a direct answer, or ask an AI assistant who to call. The new site is built for all three.

SEO: found on Google and Maps

A page for each service and town, clear page titles, a fast mobile site, a complete Google Business Profile, and the same name, number and email everywhere.

AEO: the answer to questions

A plain-language Common Questions page, written so Google and voice assistants can lift the answers directly.

GEO: recommended by AI assistants

Consistent facts across the website, Google and directories, plus reviews, so assistants like ChatGPT can confirm and recommend the business.

Why WordPress

WordPress is the world's most widely used website platform and suits local search well. Proven SEO tools handle titles, descriptions and site maps, Google Site Kit shows which searches lead to calls, and the business owns the site, content and web address outright. Almost any South African web developer can work on it, so there is no lock-in.
