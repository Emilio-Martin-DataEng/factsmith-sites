What we found

What customers and search engines can see today.

Pages on Google: only the home pageThe business can only show up for searches that one page happens to match.
Page title: just 'Secureelectricfencing'The title is what Google shows in results; it names no service or town.
Content: written for shareholdersFounding story, vision, mission and strategy statements. No services list and no call to action.
Services: listed elsewhere, no pages hereGates, garage doors, CCTV, automation, alarms, aluminium welding and tank repairs each have no page, so each is a search the business cannot win.
Security: indexed on http, not httpsBrowsers may label it 'Not secure', an awkward look for a security company.
Listings: out of dateA 2016 directory entry still shows a landline and Yahoo email; another listing says the company has been going for 10 years.
Duplicates and reviewsTwo separate Procompare profiles split reviews and rankings, and we found only one 5-star review across the listings.

The opportunity: 26 years of proof

Trading since 2000, with its own premises for manufacturing and distribution. That is the strongest trust signal the business has, and today it is buried.

A compliance certificate page for sellers, agents and conveyancers captures people who need an answer quickly.

Note on the check

The site blocks automated tools, so design and mobile layout still need a visual check.
