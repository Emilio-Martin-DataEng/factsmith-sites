Selling a property in Vanderbijlpark?

Since 1 October 2012, any property with an electric fence must have a valid Electric Fence System Certificate of Compliance before ownership can transfer. The certificate is issued by a registered electric fence installer and covers the whole system: energiser, wires, earthing, gate contacts and warning signs.
