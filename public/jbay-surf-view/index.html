Surf guide

Pick your wave from the garden.

You can see most of the JBay breaks from the house. Kurt will show you around the lineup and set you up with lessons and boards.

BoneyardsTop of the point. Lefts and rights over a shallow reef; a locals' favourite.
SupertubesSouth Africa's premier wave and one of the best rights in the world. 4 to 8 ft, rides up to half a kilometre on the right day. Best on a SW wind. Home of the July pro contest.
Impossibles ("Salads")The inside section in front of the car park, where Supers closes out. Where the brave pull in.
TubesShort, intense, barrelling. Best at 4 to 5 ft on a S or SW swell; small take-off, so it can't hold a crowd.
PointThe original 1960s longboard wave. Heavy take-off, then a carving wall. Best at 3 to 8 ft; the place to start your JBay trip.
Kitchen WindowsMellow and fun on the mid-tide, away from the busy spots.
MagnatubesHeavy and unpredictable; 3 to 5 ft on a NW wind at high tide.
AlbatrossOut of town and uncrowded. Needs a NW wind and low tide to come good.
When to comeSW swells from the Roaring Forties run March to September; July and August are the most consistent, with weeks of head-high to double-overhead swell. Summer brings warm water and onshore easterlies.
What to bringTwo boards: a 6'0" to 6'6" for smaller days and a 6'10" to 7'6" for the big stuff. A full suit most of the year, and booties against the mussel-covered rocks at Supers.
Respect the line-upSupers is the most localised spot in the country. Wait your turn, watch how the locals paddle out, and the waves will come.

Questions guests ask

How far is JBay Surf View from Supertubes?

About 150 m down to the beach, then roughly five minutes' walk along the sand to Supertubes.

Is it self-catering?

Yes. Each of the three flats has its own kitchen or kitchenette, private entrance and braai or fire area.

Which flat suits a family?

Salads sleeps up to 6, with a double room plus a room with a double bed and bunks. Tubes sleeps 4 in two en-suite bedrooms.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from TBC, check-out by TBC. Kurt checks you in personally.

Is there DSTV?

No. Tubes and Point have a smart or wall-mounted TV, Wi-Fi and an HDMI cable, so you can stream Netflix or YouTube from your laptop. Salads has a TV in the double room. Wi-Fi in Salads TBC

When is the best surf in Jeffreys Bay?

March to September brings the big south-west swells, and July and August are the most consistent. Supertubes is at its best from 4 to 8 ft on a south-west wind.

What boards and wetsuit should I bring?

Two boards: a 6'0" to 6'6" and a 6'10" to 7'6". A full wetsuit for most of the year, and booties for the rocks at Supers. Boards can also be hired here.

How do I get to Jeffreys Bay?

Fly into Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) via Johannesburg or Cape Town. Kurt can arrange an airport taxi for R800.

Can I get a chiropractic treatment during my stay?

Yes. Dr Kurt is a sports chiropractor and offers in-house consults, plus arranged massage and rehab programmes.
