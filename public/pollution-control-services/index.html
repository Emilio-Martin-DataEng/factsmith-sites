Common questions

Short answers to what clients ask most.

Do you offer 24-hour emergency response?

Yes. Our hazmat response unit is available 24 hours a day for spills, sewer overflows and blockages. Call or WhatsApp 065 145 9199.

What is a super sucker?

A super sucker is a high-powered vacuum truck. Ours use a 6-inch liquid ring pump and carry 10,000 litres, strong enough to lift thick sludge, grit and settled solids from ponds, clarifiers and pump stations.

What is the difference between a super sucker and a honey sucker?

A honey sucker is built for liquid waste such as septic and conservancy tanks. Ours carry 14,000 litres with B50 vacuum pumps. A super sucker has far more suction for heavy sludge and solids.

Do you issue safe disposal certificates?

Yes. Waste is taken to licensed disposal sites and the paperwork is kept per job. confirm certificates and manifests issued per job
