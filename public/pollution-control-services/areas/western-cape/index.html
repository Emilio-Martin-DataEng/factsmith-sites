What we do in Western Cape

Along the Overberg and Garden Route coast we work with Overstrand and Bitou on pump stations, sewer lines and wastewater plants, where tourism season pushes systems to their limit.

Super sucking

Our 6-inch liquid ring super sucker trucks clean water treatment plants, wastewater treatment works, pump stations, anaerobic and settling ponds, clarifiers and related infrastructure.

High-pressure jetting

Hydro jetting clears and maintains sewer lines, stormwater lines and the whole drainage system, restoring flow without digging.

Desludging and honey sucking

Removal of sludge and waste from septic tanks, conservancy tanks and process tanks, so the system keeps running at full capacity.
