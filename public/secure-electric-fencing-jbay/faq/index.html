Common questions

Straight answers from the people who install and repair the fences.

Do I need an electric fence certificate to sell my house?

Yes. Since October 2012 a valid Electric Fence System Certificate of Compliance is required before a property with an electric fence can transfer. A registered installer inspects the system and issues it.

How much does an electric fence cost in Jeffreys Bay?

It depends on the length of the boundary, the number of strands, the energiser and the gates. Most homes are quoted per metre plus the energiser and battery. We quote free on site. Add a typical price range once confirmed

Why does my electric fence keep beeping?

The beeping is the energiser telling you something is wrong: usually a short from plants or a broken wire, a weak battery after load-shedding, or a poor earth. Check for anything touching the wires, then call us if it continues.

Does salt air affect an electric fence?

Yes. On the coast, standard fittings rust and wires lose tension faster. We use corrosion-resistant hardware and recommend a yearly service.

Can you look after my fence while I am away?

Yes. Our maintenance plans include scheduled checks, battery replacement and call-outs, so the fence works when you are not there.
