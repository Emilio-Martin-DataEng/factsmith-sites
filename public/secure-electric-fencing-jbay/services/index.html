Services

Our services

One local team for installation, repairs, certificates and maintenance.

Home installations

Wall-top and freestanding electric fences designed around your property, with energiser, battery backup, warning signs and earthing. We can link the fence to your alarm.

  • Wall-top brackets on existing walls
  • Freestanding fences on open boundaries
  • Alarm and siren integration
  • Strand count to suit the wall and the risk

Repairs and call-outs

Fence beeping, tripping or dead? We trace the fault, from shorts and broken wires to tired batteries and failed energisers, and fix it on the spot where we can.

  • Fault finding and short tracing
  • Battery and energiser replacement
  • Broken or slack wire repair
  • Storm and lightning damage

Gate electrification

Electric fencing carried across sliding and swing gates, with gate contacts so the gate opens normally and the fence stays live.

  • Gate contacts and flexible bridging
  • Integration with existing gate motors

Compliance certificates

Inspection, repairs if needed, and the Electric Fence System Certificate of Compliance for property transfers. How certificates work

Maintenance plans

Scheduled visits to test voltage, clear vegetation, check earthing and replace batteries before they fail. Ideal for holiday homes, rentals and complexes.

  • Monthly or quarterly visits
  • Priority call-outs confirm terms

Business, complexes and smallholdings

Larger perimeters for shops, complexes, light industry and smallholdings around Humansdorp and the Kouga farms.

  • Zoned systems so one fault does not drop the whole fence
  • Longer runs and higher strand counts
