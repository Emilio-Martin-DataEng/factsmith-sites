What customers and search engines can see today.

Reviews: noneFacebook shows 306 likes and no reviews, so new customers have no proof of good work.
Google: no Jeffreys Bay Business Profile foundThe business is missing from Google Maps and near-me searches, where most local jobs start.

The opportunity: compliance certificates

A valid Electric Fence Certificate of Compliance is required before a property transfer can go through, and Jeffreys Bay has a busy market of holiday homes, rentals and resales.

Sellers, estate agents and conveyancers search for this in a hurry. National companies publish explainer pages. No Jeffreys Bay business we found does.

Local competition

Most local competitors (Bolt Secure Electric Fencing, Tzitzikamma Fencing, Hometechsa, JHL Security Products) appear only as short entries on directory sites, not with websites of their own. The top spot is still up for grabs.
