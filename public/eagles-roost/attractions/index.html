Attractions

Beaches, the river and the gorge — plus family days out, adventure parks, guided fishing and plenty of places to eat, all within a short drive of the house.

Guided fishingSalt CartelFishing stayStay packages

Umtentweni Main Beach

5 minutes from Eagle's Roost

Golden sand, a tidal pool, safe swimming and lifeguards in season, plus shady picnic and braai spots.

Photo: South Coast Explore

Umtentweni Whale Deck

5 minutes from Eagle's Roost

Community lookout deck run by the Umtentweni Conservancy for humpback and southern right whales migrating May to December.

Photo: Visit KZN South Coast

Umzimkhulu River Kayaking

10 minutes from Eagle's Roost

Guided or solo kayak trips on the estuary, based at Spillers Wharf.

Photo: Visit KZN South Coast

Spillers Waterfront

10 minutes from Eagle's Roost

Shops, restaurants, coffee, a bookstore and a craft shop on the banks of the Umzimkhulu in Port Shepstone.

Photo: Visit KZN South Coast

Oribi Gorge Nature Reserve

25 minutes from Eagle's Roost

Hiking, ziplining and the world's highest gorge swing, with cliff-top viewpoints and waterfalls inland.

Photo: Wild 5 Adventures

Port Shepstone Country Club

10 minutes from Eagle's Roost

18-hole championship golf course dating back to 1912, one of several within easy reach.

Photo: Port Shepstone Country Club

Granny's Pool, Umtentweni

5 minutes from Eagle's Roost

The sheltered tidal pool at Umtentweni main beach — calm, shallow water that is a firm family favourite for little ones and nervous swimmers.

Upside Down House

25 minutes from Eagle's Roost

A walk-through house built completely upside down at Margate — a quirky photo stop the kids love, on the way to the beachfront.

Mac Banana

30 minutes from Eagle's Roost

Working banana farm and family adventure park — farm stall, tractor tours, tube slides and mini-golf inland at Bendigo.

Wild Coast Sun

45 minutes from Eagle's Roost

Casino, resort and the Wild Waves water park just over the border at Port Edward — a full day out on the doorstep of the Wild Coast.
